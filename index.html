    Step 1 · Product and code

    Petrol cars of 1,500–3,000 cc, HS 870323

    India’s exports of this code fell from $1.95bn in 2019 to $0.41bn in 2025. This plan rebuilds a niche in one market rather than scaling a strong position.

    Step 2 · India’s edge

    Small, low-priced, and no revealed advantage

    India’s share of world trade in this code is 0.185% and its RCA is 0.10. The edge has to come from price and from right-hand-drive markets where India already sells.

    India’s exports, $ million

    RCA, worked out

    RCA = (India product exports ÷ India total exports) ÷ (world product exports ÷ world total exports). Below 1 means India is less specialised here than the world average.

    Top 3 rival exporters

    Overlap is the share of India’s 2025 export value that meets this rival in Mexico and Australia (58% of India’s exports), weighted by the rival’s share of those markets’ imports. It stands in for the ITC export similarity index, which I could not download.
